## 2. Description of Service

Wellet is a Personal Health Record (PHR) application designed for family caregivers. It helps you organize, store, and understand health information for the people in your care — we call them your "care recipients."

**Core features include:**

- Storing and organizing health records, medications, appointments, and care notes for your care recipients;
- AI-powered features: "Ask Wellet" (conversational health Q&A) and "Update Me" (AI-generated care summaries);
- Care Circle: sharing health summaries with other trusted family members or caregivers (available on the Plus and Pro plans);
- EHR import: importing health records from electronic health record systems (available on the Pro plan).

Wellet is a care coordination and health information management tool. It is not a medical device, a healthcare provider, or an emergency service.

## 7. AI-Powered Features

Wellet offers two AI-powered features:

- **Ask Wellet:** A conversational interface where you can ask questions about health information, medications, conditions, and caregiving topics to help you prepare for appointments and understand your loved one's care.
