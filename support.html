Common questions

My hospital didn't connect. What now?

Most hospitals running Epic or Cerner can connect to Wellet. If yours didn't show up or the connection failed, use the "Request a hospital" link inside the app — it sends us the hospital name and the error, and we'll work on adding it.

In the meantime, you can upload a MyChart export or a discharge summary PDF and Wellet will still organize the information for you.

I didn't get the magic-link sign-in email.

Magic links go out within a few seconds via Brevo. If you don't see it:

1. Check your spam or "Promotions" folder. The first one from us often lands there.
2. Make sure you typed the email correctly (one character off and the link goes nowhere).
3. Email support@getwellet.com and we'll resend manually.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Settings, and tap "Delete my account." We hold your data for 30 days in case you change your mind, then permanently delete everything within 45 days of the original request.

You can also email privacy@getwellet.com with "Delete my account" in the subject and we'll do it for you.

Can I share access with another family member?

Yes. Invite them through the Care Circle settings on your loved one's profile. They'll get a text or email with a sign-in link tied to your loved one's record. Everyone in a Care Circle sees the same chart, and you can remove someone at any time.

Is Wellet HIPAA-compliant?

Wellet is a Personal Health Record (PHR) app you use directly — not a healthcare provider, health plan, or insurer — so HIPAA doesn't apply to us the way it applies to a hospital. We say so plainly rather than make a misleading claim.

What we do commit to: TLS in transit, AES-256 at rest, Row-Level Security so only you and your Care Circle see your loved one's data, magic-link sign-in (no passwords to leak), and the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule. Full details in our privacy policy and your data page.

Does Wellet give medical advice?

No. Wellet surfaces verbatim public information — FDA drug labels, NIH-listed clinical trials, NCI-designated centers, patient advocacy groups, and PubMed citations — and lets you decide what to do with it. We don't diagnose, recommend, or interpret. Anything that looks like a suggestion is something to bring to your loved one's care team.

What does Wellet Connect do?

Wellet Connect is the iPhone companion app. It reads the Apple Health data types you authorize (steps, heart rate, HRV, sleep, weight, workouts) and sends them to your Wellet account so vitals sit alongside the hospital chart. It's a one-way bridge — Wellet never writes back to Apple Health.

You can revoke access at any time from iPhone → Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Wellet Connect.

I found a bug or have a feature idea.

Use the in-app feedback form (gear icon → "Send feedback") or email support@getwellet.com. Every message is read by a human, usually the same person who built the feature you're writing about.

How much does Wellet cost?

Wellet is free during early access. When we introduce paid plans, current users will get advance notice and a meaningful grace period. We will never sell your data — that's not how we plan to fund this.

Where is Wellet built?

By a small team in North Carolina, started by a daughter who couldn't find the same answer twice while caring for her mom. We're not VC-backed yet, not selling ads, and not a clinical tool — we're a record that belongs to your family, not the hospital.

Still stuck? Email support@getwellet.com with as much detail as you can — what you tried, what happened, what you expected. We'll write back.
