Wellet brand & UX system

A calm field guide for the people doing the caring.

This is the v2 reference library — colors, type, components, photography, and the patterns that hold them together across getwellet.com, mywellet.com, and pro.mywellet.com. Witness, don't monitor.

Voice in one paragraph

Plain, warm, evidence-based. We don't perform empathy and we don't perform tech. We notice patterns over time, hold context, and surface the right thing at the right moment — then let the caregiver decide. Sentences short enough to read on a phone at 11pm.

We say

  • Witness, don't monitor
  • What changed · What's next · What to ask
  • Worth confirming
  • You decide

We never say

  • Track, monitor, surveillance, alerts
  • Earn, guaranteed, AI-powered, ML-driven
  • HIPAA-compliant (as a marketing line)
  • Always-on, real-time

Backgrounds

Wellet ships with four canonical full-bleed backgrounds. The wordmark inverts based on the surface — Forest on light, Cream on dark. Never combine wordmark and background outside these four pairings.

Forest #11443B Wordmark in Cream. Primary hero surface. Use for product moments, brand films, decisive headers.
Cream #E7E7DF Wordmark in Forest. The default canvas. Use for editorial, body content, the calm middle.
Mist #B7C9CD Wordmark in Forest. Use for how-it-works panels, explanatory sections, secondary surfaces.
Midnight #183241 Wordmark in Cream. Reserve for serious moments — privacy, security, end-of-life content.
Pairing rules. Forest and Midnight take the Cream wordmark. Cream and Mist take the Forest wordmark. No other combinations. Photography overlays live on top of Forest or Midnight only.

The wordmark

  • Custom-drawn vector — not type-set
  • Lowercase, single weight, one color
  • Color: Forest #11443b on Cream #e7e7df
  • Source file: assets/wellet-wordmark.svg
  • Minimum height: 20px digital, 8mm print
  • Clear space: at least the height of the wordmark on every side
  • No italic. No accent dot. No orange. No outline.

CareSignal states

Three states. Never more. Stable = nothing to do. Watching = a pattern worth holding. Attention = a real anomaly that needs the caregiver's eyes today.

Stable

Mist · status.stable. No banner, no count, no nudge.

Watching

Mint · status.watching. Surfaces in weekly summary. No push notification.

Attention

Alert · status.attention. One push at most. Always actionable.

Person-tinted background system

The full content surface tints to a per-person hue. Top bar and bottom tab bar stay white. Tints sit within 4–6% lightness of one another — a peripheral cue, not a billboard. Sampled directly from the live app via getComputedStyle.

Timeline & records list

Vertical rail with circular nodes. Filter chips (All · EHR · Voice · Documents · Care circle) above. Each entry has an icon-tile eyebrow tag, Gambetta title, and provenance footer.

Detail screens · sourced sections

Every medical fact appears under an eyebrow naming its source — OPENFDA, MEDLINEPLUS, EHR. This is a safety requirement, not a style preference.

Ask Wellet & side menu

Mic always present (voice is a first-class input). Seed prompts populate but don’t auto-send. The w-mark appears here as a chrome icon — the only place it does.

Don'ts

No orange italic wordmark. Terra is retired. The wordmark is Forest, Gambetta Medium, lowercase, no italic, no dot.
No "AI-powered," "ML-driven," "real-time," or "always-on." If you'd put it on a billboard at CES, don't put it in Wellet.
No HIPAA-compliance as a marketing line. State practices instead — encryption at rest, you control the data, deletable on request.
No invented eligibility numbers. Every program rule must cite a source, or be marked NEEDS VERIFIED SOURCE.
No alarm-style red. Alert is for actual error states only. Not for highlights, not for accent rules, not for badges.
No stock smiles. Photography is hands, objects, and quiet rooms. Never a couple grinning at a tablet.
No more than one CareSignal push per day. Witness, don't monitor.
No Mint as a body fill. Mint is a highlight: one small shape, one underline, one dot.
