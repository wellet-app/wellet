How Wellet works

Wellet turns scattered health records into one clear picture — so you can stop being the memory and start being the advocate.

1

Upload what you have

Drop in a discharge summary, lab result, medication list, or insurance card. Take a photo of a prescription bottle. Wellet reads it, extracts the details, and adds it to the health timeline.

  • PDFs, photos, and documents — parsed automatically by AI
  • Voice recordings from doctor visits — transcribed and summarized
  • MyChart health record exports (ZIP files) — imported in seconds
2

Connect their patient portal

Link through SMART on FHIR — the same secure standard hospitals use. Wellet reads medications, conditions, allergies, and lab results directly. Read-only. The provider’s records are never modified.

  • Works with Epic MyChart and other FHIR-enabled portals
  • Read-only access — Wellet never writes to the EHR
  • The person you care for authorizes the connection directly
3

Get your Update Me summary

Update Me gives you a plain-language health summary whenever you need one — what changed, what to watch, what to bring up at the next appointment. No jargon. No portal-hopping.

  • AI summaries generated from actual records
  • Highlights changes since the last visit
  • Share with siblings and other caregivers so everyone’s on the same page
4

Ask anything

Ask Wellet anything about the health history you’ve captured. “What medications are they taking for blood pressure?” “When was the last CBC?” Answers grounded in their actual chart — not the internet.

  • Answers drawn from records you’ve uploaded or connected
  • Understands context across medications, labs, and appointments
  • Works in plain language — no medical training required
5

Track patterns, not just events

Wellet watches for changes across vitals, labs, medications, and wearable data — and surfaces trends before they become emergencies. CareSignals connects Apple Health, Oura, and home sensors.

  • Blood pressure trends, sleep patterns, medication timing
  • Wearable data from Apple Watch, Oura, and more
  • Alerts when something changes from baseline
6

Coordinate your care circle

Caregiving is rarely a solo job. Share the health timeline with siblings, aides, and providers — so everyone who needs to know, knows.

  • Invite family members and caregivers by email
  • Control who sees what
  • Share visit summaries directly from the app
7

Be ready for anything

Emergencies don’t wait. One tap opens the ER summary — everything a doctor needs to treat the person you care for, even if you’re not in the room. And before every appointment, Wellet generates targeted questions from recent health changes.

  • One-tap ER summary with medications, conditions, allergies, and contacts
  • AI-generated visit questions based on recent changes
  • Available even before you log in — for true emergencies

Built for the person holding someone else's chart

Every health tool points at "me." Caregiving points outward. Wellet is the first health platform designed for family caregivers.
