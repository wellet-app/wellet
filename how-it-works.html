How Wellet works

Wellet turns scattered health records into one clear picture — so you can stop being the memory and start being the advocate.

1

Upload what you have

Drop in a discharge summary, lab result, medication list, or insurance card. Take a photo of a prescription bottle. Wellet reads it, extracts the details, and adds them to your loved one's health timeline.

  • PDFs, photos, and documents — parsed automatically by AI
  • Voice recordings from doctor visits — transcribed and summarized
  • MyChart health record exports (ZIP files) — imported in seconds
2

Connect their patient portal

Link your family member's MyChart or other patient portal through SMART on FHIR — the same secure standard hospitals use. Wellet reads medications, conditions, allergies, and lab results directly. Read-only. Your provider's records are never modified.

  • Works with Epic MyChart and other FHIR-enabled portals
  • Read-only access — Wellet never writes to the EHR
  • Your family member authorizes the connection directly
3

Ask questions, get answers

Ask Wellet anything about your loved one's health in plain language. "What medications is Mom taking for her heart?" "When was her last CBC?" Wellet answers from the records you've uploaded — no more digging through binders.

  • Answers grounded in your family member's actual records
  • Prepare questions before doctor visits
  • Understand what lab results mean in context
4

Stay updated without the phone tag

Update Me gives you a plain-language health summary whenever you need one — what changed, what to watch, what to ask the doctor. Share it with your care circle so everyone sees the same picture.

  • AI health summaries tailored to your family member
  • Share summaries with siblings and other caregivers
  • Pattern detection — spot trends before they become emergencies

Built for the person holding someone else's chart

Every health tool points at "me." Caregiving points outward. Wellet is the first health platform designed for family caregivers.
